What we fix

Complete auto repair

Front-end and brake work is most of what comes through the door. If you need something that isn't here, call and ask.

CV joints & axles

We replace worn CV joints and complete axle shafts.

Signs: clicking or popping on turns, or grease slung inside the wheel.

Wheel bearings

Bearing and hub assembly replacement.

Signs: a hum or growl that gets louder the faster you go.

Brakes

New pads and rotors when yours are worn down.

Signs: squealing, grinding, or a shake in the wheel when you stop.

Shocks & struts

Worn shocks and struts replaced so the car rides level again.

Signs: bouncing after bumps, or the nose diving when you brake.

Front wheel alignments

We set the front wheels straight so the car tracks true.

Signs: pulling to one side, or tires wearing on one edge.

Oil changes

Oil and filter changes.

Signs: you're due by mileage, or the oil light is on.

Not sure what's wrong?

What's that noise?

Most front-end problems tell on themselves. Here is what the common ones usually mean.

  • Clicking when you turnUsually a worn CV joint.
  • A hum that grows with speedPoints to a wheel bearing.
  • Squealing or grinding to a stopBrake pads, and maybe rotors.
  • Bouncing after every bumpWorn-out struts.
  • Pulling to one sideThe front end is out of alignment.
